# Light Painting

**Family:** Light & Imaging · **id:** `light-painting`

**Look.** A long exposure in a dark field: a starry sky with lengthening star trails, a treeline silhouette, warm horizon bokeh and wet grass. Everything is drawn in the air with an LED wand. Trails are hand-jittered with white-hot cores and coloured falloff. A twisting RGB light-tube ribbon loops the hub. A translucent ghost of the painter builds up as the shutter stays open.

**Motion.** The wand tip (a bright spark with a star cross) traces each element live. Handwriting is revealed behind it, the ribbon sweeps around the hub, and star trails grow over the 12 seconds.

**How it explains.** Each beat is drawn on with the wand: boxes are traced frames whose end overshoots the start, arrows are light trails with traced heads, and labels are handwriting in light. Numbers come from an LED pixel stick (5x7 dot glyphs with a rainbow sweep across the columns). The emphasis is a traced ring with a spark burst where it closes. The scene change is a whip-pan that smears every light into streaks.

**Palette.** sky `#05070f`, cyan `#4ff3ff`, magenta `#ff4fd8`, amber `#ffb347`, lime `#9dff6a`, violet `#9b7bff`, white `#fff7ea`.

**Fonts.** Caveat 600/700 (light handwriting).

**Signature techniques.**
- Trails are three additive (`lighter`) passes (wide faint, mid colour, thin white core) over a `K.wobble` hand jitter.
- Text is double-exposed a hair apart and revealed by a clip that follows the wand.
- The ribbon is 22 thin strands, hue across the width, twisting around an ellipse.
- The finish casts colour spill onto the grass from a flipped, squashed, blurred low-res copy of the painting, then applies two bloom passes, a vignette and grain.

**SFX.** bed `space` + `room`, a soft `scribble` (spray) while the wand moves, rising `pluck`s per label, a `riser` under the ribbon, a big `whoosh` whip-pan, `count`, `ding` on the ring, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Magical or creative topics, "how it connects" diagrams, AI and neural-network explainers, anything that should feel hand-made but luminous.
