# Radiograph on Lightbox

**Family:** Light & Imaging · **id:** `x-ray`

**Look.** Blue-base X-ray film clipped to a fluorescent viewer. Subjects are additive translucent density: hollow shells brighter at the edges than inside, near-white metal screws and pivot pins, soft plastics. The film has a collimation border with a soft penumbra, an "R PA" lead marker, edge print, and baked dust and scratches. The radiologist works it in waxy yellow grease pencil, with headers on masking tape.

**Motion.** The viewer's tubes stutter on and the film develops top-down from milky fog. The camera pushes in slowly. Grease pencil writes and circles. The ID flasher types the number into its white block. Gears turn very slightly.

**How it explains.** "Your video" is a radiograph of a phone (battery cell, logic board, lens ring, screws, play glyph). Creator OS is a gear train with a spring and controller chip under a masking-tape tag. Arrows, labels, boxes and the emphasis loop (with a tick) are grease pencil on the glass. The counter is typed into the ID flash block. The scene change pulls the film down out of the clips and slaps a new film up with a bounce, while the clips stay fixed on the viewer.

**Palette.** viewer `#e9eef2`, film `#07111c`, exposed `#03070d`, border `#3c5163`, bone `#cfe9ff`, grease `#f4d23c`, tape `#e8dcb8`, ink `#1d1b18`.

**Fonts.** Permanent Marker (marker on tape), Caveat 700 (grease pencil), Courier Prime 700 (ID flash, counter).

**Signature techniques.**
- Density is drawn with `lighter` plus a soft same-colour `shadowBlur`: a low-alpha fill with a brighter shell stroke reads as hollow bone or plastic.
- Cached viewer plate (tube banding, bezel) and film plates (base, collimation, marker, edge print, dust, scratches).
- Grease pencil is three offset, streaky passes. Tape has torn edges and fibre lines.
- The finish adds the push-in, halation bloom off the bright viewer, a glass sheen, the fluorescent stutter at start, a vignette and grain.

**SFX.** bed `hum` (ballast) + `room`, `click` + `glitch` tube stutter, `paper` for tape, `scribble` (pencil, marker) matched to each draw, `tick` per label, `paper` + `whoosh` + `thud` film swap, `type` + `count` on the ID flasher, `pop` on the tick, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Diagnoses and teardowns, "what is inside" explainers, audits, before/after findings, anything framed as an expert reading the evidence.
