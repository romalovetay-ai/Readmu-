# Punk Zine Collage

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `collage-zine`

**Look.** A photocopied cut-and-paste zine spread on off-white copy paper, crushed to high-contrast black toner with speckle and white dropouts. A torn fluoro-pink band is multiplied across the sheet, off-register. Titles are ransom-note letters, each a different magazine face on its own scrap, with typed strips under masking tape, torn halftone scraps with staples and black marker scrawls on top.

**Motion.** The toner texture cycles through four speckle tiles at 8 fps, and ransom letters and marker arrows jitter on that same clock, so the whole sheet boils like cheap stop-motion. A faint grey drum band wanders down the page. Labels and scraps slam down slightly oversized and settle. Each beat is a new photocopy slapped over the last.

**How it explains.** Titles and counters are cut from letter scraps in mixed faces, cases, colours and tilts (paper, black, pink or grey backgrounds). Labels are typewriter strips with a taped corner. Arrows are doubled black marker lines with two barbs. Boxes are torn halftone scraps with tape and a staple. Emphasis is a fat pink loop plus a double underline multiplied over the text. The scene change slams a fresh copied sheet in with a copier flash bar, then yanks it off to the left.

**Palette.** paper `#f2f0ea`, toner `#111111`, spot pink `#ff2e88`, grey `#d8d4cb`, masking tape `rgba(222,204,160,0.72)`, strip fill `#fbfaf6`.

**Fonts.** Ransom letters cycle through Anton, Abril Fatface, Black Ops One, Bebas Neue, Shrikhand, Rubik Mono One, Playfair Display 900 and Special Elite. Special Elite is also the typed label face, and Permanent Marker is listed for scrawl.

**Signature techniques.**
- Per-glyph random font, size, case, background scrap and rotation from a seeded generator, re-jittered on the 8 fps step; long lines are scaled to fit.
- Torn polygons built from jittered edge segments, filled with a coarse halftone dot pattern and a gradient wash.
- Pink spot ink applied with `multiply` compositing.
- Finish stacks a toner tile pattern, overlay grey contrast crush and grain.

**SFX.** beds `tape` and `room`, rapid `paper` rustles, `stamp` as scraps slam down, `type` on strips, `scribble` with the marker medium, `whoosh` + `riser` on the sheet swap, `count` for the numeral, `glitch` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Loud, rebellious, anti-polish messaging, hype or manifesto-style pieces, music and youth-culture brands.
