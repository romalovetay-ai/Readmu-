# Ballpoint Notebook

**Family:** Hand-Drawn · **id:** `ballpoint-notebook`

**Look.** A blue Bic doodle on college-ruled notebook paper: pale cream sheet, light blue rules, a double red margin line, three punched holes showing a dark desk behind, and small star, zigzag, cube and spiral doodles in the margin, each traced over several times. Titles are Fredoka bubble letters outlined in ink with a hatched drop shadow.

**Motion.** Lines are drawn on with a ballpoint whose width and darkness follow seeded pressure, with an ink blob where each stroke starts. Bubble titles are revealed left to right by the pen, with the hatched shadow filling in later. Boxes and arrows are traced twice, the second pass looser. The finish adds a soft desk-lamp falloff and fine paper grain.

**How it explains.** Labels are written in Gloria Hallelujah handwriting letter by letter. Arrows are wobbly curved strokes retraced and finished with a hand-drawn triangle head. Boxes are double-traced outlines with a crosshatched shadow down and to the right. Counters are big bubble numerals with a cross-hatched shadow. Emphasis is a red pen scribbling three looping ovals, like circling something during a boring lecture. The scene change tears the page out of the pad along a ragged edge that sweeps right to left, revealing a clean ruled page.

**Palette.** paper `#fbfbf6`, ink blue `#1f3a9c`, dark ink `#16286e`, red pen `#c4302b`, rule blue about `#5a96d2` (drawn at reduced opacity), margin red `rgba(215,70,80,0.6)`, punched-hole grey `#3b3f46`.

**Fonts.** Fredoka 600/700 (bubble titles and counters), Gloria Hallelujah (handwritten labels).

**Signature techniques.**
- A pressure-varied pen stroke with a round blob at the start, used for every line, arrow and doodle.
- Bubble lettering filled with paper colour, stroked in ink, with a shadow made from a repeating hatch tile pattern fill.
- Crosshatching of a clipped region in several angle passes, with jittered spacing, alpha and width.
- A cached page layer (ruling, margin, holes, doodles) with two variants for before and after the tear.

**SFX.** bed `room`, `scribble` with the pen medium for every stroke, `tick` as items land, `paper` + `whoosh` for the page tear, `count` for the numeral, `pluck` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Casual, relatable explainers, study-style breakdowns and "napkin sketch" ideas that should feel like a bored doodle with a point.
