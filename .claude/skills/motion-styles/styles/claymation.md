# Claymation Set

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `claymation`

**Look.** A plasticine stop-motion still. A teal clay wall is smeared with thumb drags and knife marks and shot out of focus, in front of a lumpy terracotta clay table. Slabs and letters are chunky, waxy and bevelled, with a darker core shadow, lighter top-left sheen, faint thumbprint ridges and tool scratches. Letters are each a different bright clay colour.

**Motion.** Lumpy silhouettes re-squish every other frame ("boil on twos", 12 changes per second), and letters jitter slightly each boil. Slabs and letters pop in with squash and stretch. A clay buddy in the demo blinks, waves and hops. Grain in the finish pass also ticks at stop-motion cadence, with a warm key light from the top left.

**How it explains.** Titles and labels are pressed clay letters that squash in one at a time. Arrows are rolled coils with a clay triangle head that pops on at the end. Boxes are lumpy rounded slabs that squash in from their base. Counters are big clay numerals. Emphasis is a red clay ring that squashes on around the number. The scene change is a slab of orange clay rolled across the frame by a wooden rolling pin, covering at the midpoint and rolling off the other side.

**Palette.** wall `#4d8a96`, table `#c47447`, yellow `#f2c14e`, red `#d9503f`, teal `#3fae9a`, blue `#4f74c8`, pink `#e98aa8`, green `#7cbf5a`, cream `#f3e6cc`, orange `#ec8a3c`, ink `#2a2420`.

**Fonts.** Fredoka 500/600/700 (all clay lettering and numerals).

**Signature techniques.**
- A lumpy rounded-rect outline built from 3D noise indexed by the boil frame, smoothed with quadratic midpoints.
- A clay shape pass stack: contact shadow, base colour, light/dark gradient, soft blurred inner rim, thumbprint ellipses, a scratch and a radial specular sheen.
- Letters drawn as a dark offset, a light offset and the base colour, with a top-edge highlight.
- Coil strokes drawn as blurred shadow, dark underside, body and highlight lines; the background wall is blurred for shallow depth of field.

**SFX.** bed `room`, many low-pitched `pop` hits as pieces squash in, `thud` on slabs and emphasis, `paper` rustle, `whoosh` for the roll, `count` for the numeral, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Playful, friendly, kid-adjacent or handmade-brand explainers where a warm tactile feel and a little character matter more than precision.
