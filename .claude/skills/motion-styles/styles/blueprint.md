# Engineering Blueprint

**Family:** Hand-Drawn · **id:** `blueprint`

**Look.** A cyanotype drafting sheet. Mottled, sun-faded Prussian-blue paper carries a faint drafting grid, a double zoned border with numbered and lettered ticks, wavy fold creases, and a title block reading "CREATOR OS MK-I". Everything drawn is white mono line-work that bleeds slightly into the blue.

**Motion.** Lines are inked by a plotter: a translucent gantry rail runs across the sheet at the pen's x position with a square pen carriage at the drawing point. Lettering is plotted one character at a time. Construction lines run past the corners first, then the outline, then centre lines, hatching and dimensions follow. The finish adds a heavy dark-blue vignette and overlay grain.

**How it explains.** Titles and labels are uppercase Space Mono with a glow, and titles get a double rule plotted underneath. Arrows are drafted leaders with an origin dot, a thin line and a slim filled head, optionally with an elbow. Boxes are outlined objects with faint extension lines, a dash-dot centre line, 45 degree section hatching in the lower part and a dimension line with a numeric callout. Counters sit in a double-framed dimension box with extension ticks. Emphasis is a gold revision cloud of scalloped arcs plus a small revision triangle marked "1". The scene change slides a fresh print of the sheet in from the right, casting a shadow on the old one.

**Palette.** paper `#1f4f8c`, line `#eef5ff`, faint `#9fc0ea`, accent gold `#ffd36b`, red `#ff9a8a`, vignette `#04122c`.

**Fonts.** Space Mono 400/700 (all lettering, zone letters, title block, dimension text).

**Signature techniques.**
- Every line is drawn twice: a wide low-alpha bleed pass under a crisp core stroke, with butt caps and mitred joins.
- A cached paper layer combines paper texture, a corner sun fade, damp blotches, a grid with heavier every fifth line, border, creases and title block.
- A plotter gantry overlay follows the pen's current end point.
- Partial scalloped arcs are revealed one by one for the revision cloud.

**SFX.** bed `hum`, `scribble` with the pen medium for strokes, `click` as items land, `type` on lettering, `paper` + `whoosh` for the sheet change, `count` for the numeral, `stamp` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Technical, systems-and-architecture explainers, "how it is built" breakdowns, specs and anything that should feel engineered and precise.
