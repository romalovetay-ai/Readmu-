# Cut-Paper Diorama

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `cut-paper`

**Look.** A layered cardstock shadow box at dusk. An indigo sheet with punched-out cream stars sits behind a lilac mountain layer and two green hill layers, each a separate scissor-cut sheet with fibre flecks, a lit top-left edge and a soft drop shadow onto the layer behind. Letters are cut from coloured stock mounted on white, and labels are cream paper tags with a punched hole.

**Motion.** The mountain and hill layers drift slowly at different parallax speeds. Letters pop up one at a time with a springy overshoot. Tags drop in from above, cards slide up into place, and arrow strips grow along their path before a folded head pops on. Grain and a light vignette sit over everything.

**How it explains.** Titles are cut-out letters, each in a different stock colour (red, orange, yellow, teal, blue, pink). Labels are paper tags. Arrows are paper strips with a folded triangle head and a crease line. Boxes are two-ply cards, a dark back sheet offset behind a coloured front sheet. Counters are one tile per digit, slightly tilted, in alternating cream and white. Emphasis is a pop-up sunburst of concentric paper ellipses (red, orange, yellow, cream) rising behind the moment. The scene change slides four layered hill-shaped sheets up through the frame and out the top.

**Palette.** sky `#3d3f7a`, sky2 `#2c2d5e`, mountain `#6c6fb0`, hill `#2f7a62`, hill2 `#3f9a6e`, cream `#f4ead2`, white `#fbf8f0`, red `#e0523f`, orange `#f2913d`, yellow `#f6c64a`, teal `#3fb3a5`, blue `#4b7fd1`, pink `#ee8fb0`, ink `#2b2540`.

**Fonts.** Archivo Black (all letters, tags and digits).

**Signature techniques.**
- A cached cardstock pattern per colour: paper texture plus random fibre strokes, used as the fill for every shape.
- A shared `sheet()` routine that gives each shape a shadow scaled by depth, a bright offset edge stroke and a darker opposite one inside a clip.
- Cut letters drawn as a thick white stroked mount behind a stock-filled glyph.
- Paper strips built by offsetting a sampled path on both sides into a polygon.

**SFX.** bed `room`, many short `paper` rustles, `pop` as pieces land, `whoosh` + long `paper` for the layers, `count` for the numeral, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Warm, storybook-style explainers, onboarding, family or education content, and anything that wants a handmade layered look with depth.
