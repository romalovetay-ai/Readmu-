# Origami Studio

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `origami`

**Look.** Folded-paper models on a seamless studio sweep. Every form is flat facets shaded consistently from one key light, with crisp crease lines, two-tone paper (saturated front, pale back) and soft blurred table shadows. A warm pool of light sits on a pale grey wall that curves into the table.

**Motion.** The key light pool slowly orbits across the sweep. A paper crane bobs and flaps its wings overhead. Panels unfold crease by crease, tent cards squash up from the table with a back ease, and the arrow head folds out at the end of its draw. The finish pass is only a soft vignette and light grain.

**How it explains.** Titles are folded ribbon banners whose tails tuck behind with fold triangles. Labels and counters are standing tent cards with a lighter fold strip on top (the counter adds a red bar). Arrows are creased two-tone paper strips with a folded head. Boxes are folded panels whose lower half rotates open about a centre crease, with a dog-ear in the back colour. Emphasis is a pleated paper fan opening behind the moment with a warm flare. The scene change is a pre-creased sheet that folds over from the right, covers at the midpoint, and folds away to the left.

**Palette.** wall `#e7e5e0`, ink `#26303a`, teal `#2aa39b` (back `#a9ddd6`), red `#df5039` (back `#f6c0b2`), yellow `#efb935` (back `#fae3a3`), blue `#3a5fb2` (back `#b3c6ec`), cream `#f2ebdb` (back `#fbf8f0`).

**Fonts.** Space Grotesk 700 (banners, tent cards, counters), Space Mono (loaded for small text).

**Signature techniques.**
- A `facet()` helper fills polygons with tones mixed toward white or near-black, so shading is a lighten/darken value per face.
- Panels, banners and the crane are built from a few triangles and quads, with light or dark crease lines between them.
- Contact shadows are blurred ellipses under each object.
- The transition draws a diagonal grid of alternately toned triangles with a shadow gradient at the moving edge.

**SFX.** bed `room`; `paper` rustles on nearly every entrance, `pluck` on the title, `pop` on labels, `whoosh` + `paper` on the transition, `count` for the counter, `ding` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Friendly, tactile explainers and product walkthroughs that should feel crafted and calm, with clear colour-coded steps.
