# Illuminated Manuscript

**Family:** Illustration · **id:** `illuminated`

**Look.** A folio from a Book of Hours. Mottled vellum with darker cockled edges, faint ruling and a column of
pricking holes down the outer margin. Text in a gothic book hand in iron-gall brown, rubrics and the first letter
of a line in vermilion. Panels are miniatures: a frame of raised burnished gold around a lapis, malachite or rose
field edged in white-lead filigree. An ivy rinceau with coloured leaves and gold bezants runs down the left border
bar and along the foot of the page, and a snail drollery crawls along it.

**Motion.** The scribe works the page. Gold leaf is laid square by square around each frame, then the pigment
pools outward from the centre of the field and the filigree is added. A quill rides the writing point while text
is written letter by letter (the newest letter is still wet). Gold carries a slow travelling glint.

**How it explains.** Folios. A champ initial opens the hook, miniatures stand for things, penwork arrows (ink line
with a vermilion hairline twin, curl flourishes, a gilded leaf head) show flow, list items are marked with
vermilion pilcrows, numbers are burnished gold numerals, and the key moment is ringed in vermilion penwork with
radiating flourishes tipped by bezants. The scene change is the leaf turning to the next folio.

**Palette.** vellum `#efe1bf`, iron-gall ink `#2b1d12`, vermilion `#b3311d`, lapis `#24408f`, rose `#b9566d`,
malachite `#2f7a4c`, gold `#c99a35` / highlight `#f6dc8a` / bole `#7d5716`, white lead `#fbf4e0`.

**Fonts.** Grenze Gotisch 700/400 (book hand, readable on a phone), IM Fell English italic (folio numbers).

**Signature techniques.**
- Raised gold: an offset bole shadow, a dark-light-dark metallic gradient and a glint band clipped with
  `source-atop` whose position is a function of t.
- Gold laid as discrete squares while drawing on, then joined into one burnished frame.
- Pigment flood as a growing disc clipped to the field, with a darker rim where the paint pooled.
- Vellum: cached fbm paper plus large soft blotches, edge darkening, ruling and pricking, warm vignette, grain.
- Cached rinceau: wobbled vine with three-lobed leaves in four pigments and radial-gradient bezants.

**SFX.** draw `pen` (quill scratch, `brush` for pigment floods), land `pluck` (rising modal plucks), emphasis
`chime`, transition `paper` + soft `whoosh` (leaf turn), bed `room`.

**When to use it.** Heritage, craft, "the definitive guide", anything that should feel like a treasured,
authoritative text or a ceremonial reveal.
