# Cosmic Star Chart

**Family:** Cosmic & Psychedelic · **id:** `cosmic`

**Look.** Deep-space painting crossed with an engraved celestial atlas. A near-black blue sky is filled with fractal-noise nebulae in teal, violet, rose and gold and a scatter of stars. Over it sit hairline gold and pale-cyan chart elements: a warm cream Syne headline with a graduated gold rule, small-caps serif labels, dotted orbits with chevrons, and double-framed cartouches. The demo's heroes are a banded, ringed gas giant with a cratered moon and a diffraction-spiked star.

**Motion.** The nebula plate drifts slowly with a gentle zoom and sideways sway, and about eighteen stars twinkle on sine pulses. Headlines wipe in left to right while the gold rule and tick marks extend. Orbit lines draw on with chevrons riding them. The finish pass is a heavy dark vignette plus fine grain.

**How it explains.** Titles are big Syne with an optional small-caps kicker above. Labels are Cormorant small caps in gold with an optional italic serif gloss below. Arrows are dotted cyan orbit lines with chevron arrowheads at the midpoint and end. Boxes are dark translucent fields with a double hairline gold frame. Counters are large gold-gradient figures over a graduated sextant arc with a cyan index needle. Emphasis is an expanding dashed cyan ring and a flaring diffraction-spike star. The scene change is a warp jump: stars smear into radial streaks and a teal-white flash covers the frame at the midpoint.

**Palette.** space `#05060f`, teal `#1f8a8f`, violet `#5b2a86`, rose `#c2457a`, gold `#e6c47a`, cyan `#a6eef2`, cream `#f4ead2`, ink `#0b0c1a`, vignette `#02030a`.

**Fonts.** Syne 700/800 (headlines and counters), Cormorant SC (small-caps chart labels and kickers), Cormorant Garamond italic (glosses).

**Signature techniques.**
- A quarter-resolution fbm nebula computed per pixel from teal, violet, rose and gold noise channels, then upscaled with a blur and scattered with stars.
- A cached gas-giant sprite with colour bands, turbulent streaks and a limb shadow, drawn between a back and a front half of the ring ellipses.
- A diffraction star made with `lighter` compositing: radial glows plus four gradient spikes.
- Warp transition from 180 random radial streaks under `lighter`.

**SFX.** bed `space`, `swell` chords on openings and the emphasis, soft `scribble` with the pen medium, `chime` on landings, rising `blip` notes on the list, `riser` + `thud` into the warp, `count` for the numeral.

**When to use it.** Big-picture, visionary or "scale" stories: galaxies of data, growth, reach, a platform ecosystem, anything that benefits from awe and a premium feel.
