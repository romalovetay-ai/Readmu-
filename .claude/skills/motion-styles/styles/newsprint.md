# Vintage Newsprint

**Family:** Print · **id:** `newsprint`

**Look.** A 1950s broadsheet on yellowed pulp. A blackletter masthead ("The Daily Creator") sits over double rules and a dateline. Below come condensed banner headlines, columns of greeked justified body copy, and a coarse round-dot halftone wire photo of a creator at a desk with a ring light. The reverse page shows faintly through the paper, edges are browned, and there is a fold crease across the middle. Ink is thin and uneven, with a red grease-pencil ring as the only colour accent.

**Motion.** Headlines are set by a left-to-right clip sweeping across them, and the photo is revealed the same way. Greeked columns fade up with the page. The demo adds a slow camera creep toward the headline. The scene change is the spinning newspaper: the whole page whirls away into the dark, then the next page whirls up and slaps flat.

**How it explains.** Titles and the counter are Oswald bold caps with a faint ink-spread shadow. Labels are Oswald medium caps. Arrows are crisp elbowed rules (horizontal, vertical, horizontal) with a dot at the start and a solid wedge head. Boxes have a heavy outer rule, a hairline inner rule and an optional dot-screen tint. Emphasis is a red grease-pencil loop drawn from four slightly offset, broken, waxy passes. Body copy is Libre Baskerville.

**Palette.** paper `#e8dab4`, ink `#211d17`, grey `#5b544a`, red `#b8261d`.

**Fonts.** UnifrakturMaguntia (blackletter masthead), Oswald (headlines, labels, numerals), Libre Baskerville (greeked body, datelines, subhead).

**Signature techniques.**
- A cached "thin press ink" tile of near-solid ink with noise-starved patches, used as the fill pattern for all lettering and rules.
- Greeked copy built from random syllable words, justified per line and cached; mirrored copies at low alpha form the show-through of the reverse page.
- A halftone wire photo generated by sampling a hand-coded darkness function at a staggered dot grid and scaling dot radius by darkness.
- Finish multiplies a yellowed paper texture, adds a warm vignette and overlay grain.

**SFX.** Bed `room` plus `vinyl` crackle. Strokes use a `pencil` scribble, landings use `click`, emphasis uses `stamp`, the transition uses `whoosh`. The demo score adds `type`, `paper`, `thud` on page slaps, `count` and a closing `chime`.

**When to use it.** News-style stories, "breaking" announcements, retrospectives, press-angle pitches and any claim framed as the headline of the day.
