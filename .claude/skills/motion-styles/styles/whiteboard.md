# Whiteboard Explainer

**Family:** Hand-Drawn

**Look.** Dry-erase marker on a glossy whiteboard. Strokes are streaky (three offset passes at different opacities,
with thin board-coloured streak gaps) and pool darker where the marker starts and lifts. Boxes and arrows are traced
twice, the second pass looser. A ghost of the last erased lesson sits in the background, and a soft ceiling-light
sheen drifts across the gloss.

**Motion.** Every stroke draws on in sequence with the marker in hand at the drawing point; lettering is written one
letter at a time with a slight per-letter tilt, like live handwriting.

**How it explains.** One board, drawn live. The diagram builds left to right (portrait: top to bottom with a centre
trunk and branches). A felt eraser wipes the board (leaving faint residue) when the story moves on; the key number is
re-written in marker and circled in red.

**Palette.** Board #f6f7f4, black #1f2833, blue #1f5fbf, red #d23a2f, green #1d8a4a.

**Font.** Kalam 400/700 (Google Fonts).

**Signature techniques.** Multi-pass streaky marker stroke with pooled ends (`marker()`), seeded wobble on every path,
double-traced boxes, cached ghost-lesson layer, eraser-wipe transition, moving sheen + light grain in `finish`.

**Sound.** `scribble` marker for every stroke (length matched to the draw-on), `pop` as labels land, `count` ticks on
the counter, `stamp` on the emphasis circle, `whoosh` + `paper` on the eraser, `chime` on the takeaway, `room` bed.

**Use it for:** process and how-it-works explainers, frameworks, step-by-step breakdowns, anything that should feel
like a person teaching live.
