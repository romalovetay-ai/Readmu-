# Felt & Patchwork

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `felt-quilt`

**Look.** A cosy patchwork quilt of printed cotton squares (dots, plaid, stripes, tiny crosses) in dusty rose, sage, mustard, denim, cream, tan and mint. Each square is puffed in the middle, darkened toward the seams, and has a dashed cream quilting line inside. On top sit fuzzy felt appliqué pieces with blanket-stitch edges, buttons sewn on with thread crosses, woven name tags and felt digits.

**Motion.** The padding breathes with a tiny slow scale pulse on the quilt. Banners squash and drop in, patches fall with a lifted shadow and a soft squash, name tags pop with an overshoot. Arrows and rings are sewn on stitch by stitch in running stitch, and a felt heart pops onto the corner of the emphasis ring. The scene change pans a differently patterned section of quilt across the frame.

**How it explains.** Titles sit on a cream felt banner edged in blanket stitch, with navy felt lettering. Labels are woven tags with coloured edge bands and little stitches at both ends. Arrows are running-stitch thread with a mustard (or other colour) button knot at the start and stitched head strokes. Boxes are teal felt patches blanket-stitched around the edge. Counters are red felt digits with a dashed thread outline. Emphasis is a navy running-stitch ring plus a red felt heart. The panning section has red felt binding with a stitched edge on both sides.

**Palette.** navy `#2b3a5c`, red `#c8473d`, mustard `#d9a53a`, sage `#7fa37a`, rose `#d98b8b`, denim `#5b7aa6`, cream `#f4ead6`, thread `#f7f0e0`, ink `#2a2a35`, teal `#3f9c94`, quilt print fabrics `#d9a0a0`, `#9fb79a`, `#e3c27a`, `#8fa6c9`, `#e8dcc4`, `#c99a7f`, `#a7c1bd`, table `#d9c7a8`.

**Fonts.** Fredoka 500/600/700 (banner titles, name tags and digits).

**Signature techniques.**
- A felt fibre tile per colour (thousands of short light and dark strokes) used as a pattern fill, plus a dotted lighter rim for the fuzzy cut edge.
- An arc-length sampler along a polyline gives stitch positions and tangents, powering both blanket stitch (edge thread plus perpendicular legs) and running stitch (shadow, body and sheen passes).
- A cached quilt layer with per-square print kind, weave lines, a radial puff gradient and dashed quilting.
- Buttons drawn with a radial highlight, ring, four holes and a thread cross.

**SFX.** bed `room`, soft `thud` and `pop` for landings, `tick` runs for stitches, `click` on knots, pen `scribble`, `whoosh` + `paper` for the pan, `count` for the numeral, `pluck` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Cosy, community-minded, handmade-brand or lifestyle stories, and gentle explainers where the pieces fit together like patches in a quilt.
