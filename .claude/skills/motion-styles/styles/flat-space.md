# Flat-Vector Science

**Family:** Popular Explainer Looks · **id:** `flat-space`

**Look.** An outline-free flat-vector science illustration. A deep navy-to-violet sky carries soft purple and blue glows, a field of tiny stars and twinkling four-point sparkles, with small banded planets drifting in the corners. Shapes use rich multi-stop gradients, flat crescent shadows and white rim highlights. Labels are white speech-bubble pills, and the Creator OS node in the demo is a cute big-eyed teal robot head with a visor and a rocket flame.

**Motion.** Sparkles twinkle on sine pulses and the corner planets slowly drift in opposite directions. Title words pop in one at a time with an overshoot, and labels, capsules and arrowheads pop with the same springy ease. Arrow dots appear one by one along the path. The robot bobs, blinks about every three seconds and flickers its flame. The finish adds a vignette and light soft-light grain.

**How it explains.** Titles are Nunito Black, word by word, with an optional yellow highlighted word and a soft shadow. Labels are white pills with a navy text, a faint inner highlight and an optional coloured dot. Arrows are chunky dotted rounded curves in sky blue with a rounded triangular head landing last. Boxes are glossy capsules with gradient fill, crescent shadow, a short white highlight and a soft glow. Counters are yellow-to-orange gradient numerals with a warm glow. Emphasis is a fading yellow ring with a burst of ten sparkles. The scene change sweeps a huge pink ringed planet across the frame until it covers it at the midpoint.

**Palette.** navy `#161b4f`, indigo `#2b2170`, violet `#5a2a8f`, teal `#38c6e0`, deep teal `#1f8fb8`, yellow `#ffd23f`, orange `#ff8a3d`, pink `#ff5fa2`, ink `#1b1f4a`, sky `#7fd8ff`, mint `#3ee0b0`, white `#ffffff`.

**Fonts.** Nunito 700/800/900 (titles, pill labels, counters).

**Signature techniques.**
- A planet routine that fills a gradient disc, draws tilted rounded bands, subtracts a flat crescent shadow and strokes a rim highlight, with a ring drawn in a back half and a front half.
- Multi-stop gradient capsules with a clipped offset crescent shadow instead of any outline.
- A cached sky of gradient, radial glows and 220 stars.
- A procedural robot head built from radial gradients, a dark visor, white eyes with pupils and a blink scale.

**SFX.** bed `space`, a soft `swell` chord, many `pop` hits as items land, rising `blip` notes, `whoosh` for the planet sweep, `count` for the numeral, `ding` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Friendly science-explainer content, product tours with a cute mascot, science and tech topics for broad audiences.
