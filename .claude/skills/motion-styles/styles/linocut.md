# Linocut Relief Print

**Family:** Print · **id:** `linocut`

**Look.** The whole frame is one pulled proof: a dense black inked block on cream rice paper with loose fibres, uneven wobbly block edges and a salty, mottled ink film. Everything visible is "cut" out of the black as paper-coloured gouge strokes, with radiating gouge rays near the edge and a carved double border. A red reduction pass adds colour, and pencil notes in the margin give an edition number (3/20), the title "One Video" and "Creator OS 2026" with a signature line.

**Motion.** A faint raking glint slides slowly across the wet ink. Lettering is cut in left to right in stepped increments, as if the knife were moving. Channels (arrows, borders) draw on by trimming the path. The demo adds a slow camera ease-in on the print.

**How it explains.** Titles are Archivo Black letters in paper colour with nicks and ink specks. Labels get a red reduction banner under the cut letters. Arrows are gouged channels with a carved two-stroke head. Boxes are panels with a red reduction fill, a cut border and parallel tone-carving slivers on the left. The counter is large cut numerals, and emphasis is a ring of red gouge slivers radiating outward. The scene change is a diagonal peel: fresh paper is laid from the bottom-right, a curled flap with shadow sweeps toward the top-left, then it peels back the same way.

**Palette.** paper `#efe6d1`, ink `#16120e`, red `#c2412c`, cut `#efe6d1`, pencil `#5d5a55`.

**Fonts.** Archivo Black (carved headlines, labels, numerals), Caveat 700 and 500 (pencilled margin notes).

**Signature techniques.**
- `gouge()` draws a sliver with two quadratic curves, pointed at both ends and widest past the middle; `channel()` chains overlapping gouges with random widths for a hand-cut line.
- A cached "salty ink" tile with noise, white specks and fine roller ridges, used as pattern fill for both black and red.
- A cached background with wobbly block edges, 64 radiating ray gouges and pencil margin text.
- Finish adds the sliding glint clipped to the block, a multiplied paper texture, vignette and grain.

**SFX.** Bed `room`. Strokes use a `pencil` scribble, landings use `thud`, emphasis uses `stamp`, the transition uses `paper`. The demo score adds `chalk` scribbles, a `count` run and a closing `chime`.

**When to use it.** Strong, graphic, craft-print stories with high contrast, such as manifestos, origin stories, pull-quote reveals and anything wanting an artisan, woodblock mood.
