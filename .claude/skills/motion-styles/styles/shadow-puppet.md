# Shadow Puppet Theatre

**Family:** Paper & Craft · **id:** `shadow-puppet`

**Look.** Black card silhouettes with punched lace cut-work, pinned joints and thin control rods, pressed against a backlit cloth screen. The cloth glows amber at the lamp hotspot and falls to red-brown at the edges, with a visible weave. A scalloped lace valance with tassels hangs along the top and a grassy ground strip runs along the bottom. Coloured gels fill the cut openings.

**Motion.** The lamp breathes with a noise-driven flicker that lightens or darkens the whole frame. Hanging plaques drop in on their strings with a back ease, then sway and settle with a spring. Frame puppets rise from below the screen on their rods. Vine arrows grow along their path. The emphasis sun rotates open.

**How it explains.** Titles are twin-string hanging plaques with ornamental ears and a punched dotted border, with lettering that glows as if cut through the card. Labels are single-string plaques. Arrows are growing vines with small leaves and a leaf-shaped head. Boxes are cut-work frames with a scalloped edge, a coloured gel window, punched holes and corner pins, rising on a rod. Counters are large plaques. Emphasis is a cut-work sun with 18 alternating rays tipped with gel dots and a lamp flare. The scene change is lace curtains that close from both sides, cover the frame at the midpoint and part again.

**Palette.** glow `#ffc35a`, amber `#f0a040`, rust `#b5521d`, edge `#3a1306`, ink `#140904`, gels teal `#2bb3a0`, red `#d8432f`, gold `#e8b62a`, blue `#5b8ee0`.

**Fonts.** IM Fell English roman and italic (all plaque lettering).

**Signature techniques.**
- A cached cloth layer combines a radial lamp gradient, warp and weft thread lines, slubs, and valance and ground cut with destination-out holes.
- Plaque lettering uses a coloured shadow glow with a warm fill so it reads as light passing through.
- Frame puppets are cached layers with the window knocked out and a translucent gel painted behind.
- The finish pass applies the flicker as a soft-light or multiply wash, a deep vignette and grain.

**SFX.** bed `room` plus a low `hum`; soft `whoosh` and `pluck` as plaques hang, short `paper` rustles, pencil `scribble` as vines grow, plucked arpeggio per platform, `paper` + `whoosh` for the curtains, `count`, `ding` + `swell` on the sun, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Storybook, folklore or fable-style explainers, and stories that benefit from warmth, silhouette clarity and a theatrical, hand-operated feel.
