# Pointillist Afternoon

**Family:** Painterly · **id:** `pointillism`

**Look.** Neo-Impressionist divisionism of a Sunday on a river bank. Sky, water, grass, a shaded tree and a sailboat are all filled with small dabs of unmixed pigment (complementary hues side by side), with the bare canvas weave peeking through. A dotted painted border frames the picture, and type is made of dabs with a pale dab halo.

**Motion.** Three variants of each colour tile alternate about eight times a second, so the dots shimmer while the overall colour holds. The sailboat drifts gently. Titles reveal left to right through a clip, and plaques build up from the left.

**How it explains.** Titles and labels are Playfair Display filled with a dab pattern, over a halo of pale dabs for legibility. Arrows are a thick dab stroke over a cream outline with a dab-filled triangle head. Boxes are rounded cream plaques ringed with fine dark dabs. Counters are large dab numerals. Emphasis is two hand-drawn ellipses, red and gold, traced around the number. The scene change re-dabs the canvas in a diagonal sweep: fresh pale dabs advance across the frame, cover it at the midpoint and recede off the far corner, with a red brush line at the front.

**Palette.** canvas `#e9dfc6`, ink blues `#1c2a7a` `#3a1f6e`, sky `#9cc3f0`, water `#2f63c8`, grass `#3e9a3a`, shade `#1f5a2a`, red `#e2442a`, gold `#f5c430`, cream `#fff6dc`, bark `#5a3a1a`.

**Fonts.** Playfair Display 800 and italic 800 (titles, labels and numbers).

**Signature techniques.**
- Each colour is a cached tile of random ellipses picked from a weighted pigment mix, tiled seamlessly and used as a canvas pattern for fills and strokes.
- Fine colours (ink, red, gold, cream) use smaller dabs for legibility.
- A cached canvas weave of thin light and dark threads sits under the dabs.
- The finish pass paints the dotted border with even-odd fill, a vignette and soft-light grain.

**SFX.** bed `ocean`; a soft `swell` chord to open, spray `scribble` for every stroke and reveal, `blip` notes as items land, `whoosh` + spray on the sweep, `count`, `ding` on the emphasis, `chime` to resolve.

**When to use it.** Gentle, artful explainers, lifestyle and wellness topics, or anything that should feel warm, handmade and visually shimmering rather than technical.
