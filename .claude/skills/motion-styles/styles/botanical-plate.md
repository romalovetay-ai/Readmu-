# Botanical Plate

**Family:** Illustration · **id:** `botanical-plate`

**Look.** A hand-coloured copperplate from a naturalist's folio. Cream rag paper with foxing freckles and faint
tide marks, a pressed plate mark (the paper inside is smoother, edged by a bevelled ridge) and an engraved double
rule. Specimens are cut in black line: tapered, swelling strokes, parallel hatching laid across each form and
cross-hatched on the shadow side. Transparent watercolour (rose, leaf green, sky, ochre) is laid loosely over the
print, slightly off register, with a darker pooled edge. Figures are numbered, captions are Latin-flavoured
italics, and the header runs a letterspaced running title with volume and plate numbers.

**Motion.** Like the plate being made: outlines are engraved first, the hatching is cut line by line, and the
colourist's tint bleeds in a beat after the line work. The bloom sways gently on its stem. Plates change under the
press roller.

**How it explains.** Things are specimens: the video is a seed pod with a play mark, Creator OS is the bloom,
and the platforms are numbered parts (in portrait, leaves branching off the stem, which doubles as the trunk of
the fan-out). Flow is fletched engraved arrows with feather barbs and a cut head, numbers sit in an engraved oval
cartouche with scrolled ends, and the key result is ringed by the colourist's loose red wash.

**Palette.** paper `#efe5cc`, ink `#2a2520`, rose `#c9707a`, leaf `#7f9a62`, sky `#8fb0b8`, ochre `#d6a957`,
red wash `#b8432f`.

**Fonts.** Libre Baskerville (italic headlines, roman header and cartouche numerals), Cormorant Garamond italic
(figure captions).

**Signature techniques.**
- Every hatch line is a lens-shaped filled cut (two quadratic curves), so it swells and tapers like a burin line.
- Hatch fields are clipped to the specimen path; the shadow side gets a second, denser cross-hatch clipped to a
  half-plane.
- Watercolour is a `multiply` fill offset by a few pixels plus a wider low-alpha stroke for the pooled edge.
- Paper, foxing, tide marks and plate mark are baked once into a cached sheet.

**SFX.** draw `pen` / `pencil` (engraving), `brush` for washes, land `pluck`, emphasis `chime` + brush stroke,
transition `whoosh` + `thud` (press roller), bed `room`.

**When to use it.** Taxonomies and anatomy ("the parts of a great post"), growth stories, slow, considered
explainers that should feel scholarly and handmade.
