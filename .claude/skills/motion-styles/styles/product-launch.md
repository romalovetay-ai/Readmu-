# Product Launch Reveal

**Family:** Popular Explainer Looks · **id:** `product-launch`

**Look.** A big-tech keynote product film. One glossy glass-topped slab sits on a near-black stage in three-quarter view, lit by a soft key pool, a rim light on the back edge, a glowing violet-pink-orange edge ring and a floor reflection. Headlines are huge, tightly tracked sans type, and the key number is a gradient with a bloom.

**Motion.** Headline words arrive one at a time with a soft rise, a fade and a blur that clears. The hero device can orbit slightly, its specular band glides across the glass, its ring draws on, and small lit nodes inside pulse. Cards rise 20 pixels as they fade in. A gleam can sweep through the counter digits.

**How it explains.** Titles are word-by-word headlines with an optional small gradient eyebrow. Labels are bold white spec lines with an optional grey sub line. Arrows are hairline callouts: a ring-dot at the source and an elbowed thin line drawn out to the label, with an optional small head. Boxes are dark glass cards with a hairline border and a top-edge highlight. Counters are big gradient numerals with a pink glow. Emphasis is a soft violet and pink bloom with a thin light ring spreading on the stage floor. The scene change is a hot vertical light sweep that flares the frame to near white at the midpoint.

**Palette.** background `#050507`, stage `#121318`, text `#f4f5f7`, dim `#8a8d96`, violet `#a46bff`, pink `#ff5fa8`, orange `#ff9a3c`, hairline `#c9ccd3`, cyan `#5fe3ff`.

**Fonts.** Inter Tight 600/800 (headlines, labels, numbers), Inter 400/500/600 (sub lines and body).

**Signature techniques.**
- The device is an extruded rounded square drawn as ten stacked offset layers for the metal side, with a gradient glass top, and tilted by a vertical squash and rotation.
- The floor reflection is the same body drawn flipped on a cached layer and faded with a gradient mask (destination-in).
- Letter spacing is tightened per text style through the canvas letterSpacing property.
- The finish pass is a heavy vignette and fine grain only.

**SFX.** bed `hum`; `swell` chord with a `riser` to open, `thud` and `click` for landings, `chime` accents, `tick` + `click` pairs per platform callout, `whoosh` + `thud` on the light sweep, `count`, then a larger `swell` and `chime` on the finish.

**When to use it.** Product announcements, feature reveals and premium launch teasers where one hero object and a bold key number carry the story.
