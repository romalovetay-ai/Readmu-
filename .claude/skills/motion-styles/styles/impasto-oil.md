# Impasto Night

**Family:** Painterly · **id:** `impasto-oil`

**Look.** A Post-Impressionist night sky in thick oil paint. Short loaded daubs follow a swirling flow field in deep blue, mid blue, sky blue and pale blue over a visible canvas weave, with a green dark foreground at the bottom. Star halos made of concentric rings of yellow and cream daubs sit near the edges. Text, arrows and panels are fat daubs in chrome yellow and vermilion.

**Motion.** A few hundred extra daubs ride the swirl, fading in and out over their lifetime, so the sky keeps turning. The star halo rings pulse and rotate slowly in opposite directions. Strokes (arrows, box outlines, rings) are laid down daub by daub along their path, and lettering is revealed with a left-to-right wipe. The demo adds a very slow camera drift and zoom.

**How it explains.** Titles and labels are Fraunces black italic filled with a broken-colour paint tile, with a dark shadow and a thin light edge. Arrows are a trail of daubs with a two-daub head. Boxes are rounded fills of navy paint outlined by a daubed rectangle. The counter is large paint lettering, and emphasis is a vermilion daubed ellipse followed by two pulsing outer rings. The scene change is a palette-knife drag: a smear of mixed blue paint covers the frame left to right, then is scraped off, with a dark blade edge and a light ridge.

**Palette.** night `#14286b`, blue `#2650b0`, sky `#4f7fd0`, pale `#a9c8ee`, yellow `#f2c230`, chrome `#ffd84a`, vermilion `#e2442a`, green `#2f5f3a`, dark `#0c1636`, cream `#f6e9b8`.

**Fonts.** Fraunces italic 900 for all text.

**Signature techniques.**
- Every `daub()` is three strokes: a darker offset shadow, the body colour, and a thin lit ridge on the upper left, which reads as raised paint.
- A cached sky plate built from thousands of daubs, with colour chosen by fractal noise and angle taken from a vortex flow field.
- Seamless 96px paint tiles of random daubs used as fill patterns for text and panels.
- Finish pass adds a warm-to-cool soft-light varnish gradient, a heavy vignette and overlay grain.

**SFX.** Bed `space`. Strokes use the `brush` scribble, labels land with `pluck`, emphasis uses `thud`, the transition uses `whoosh`. The demo score adds `swell` chords, `count` for the number and a closing `chime`.

**When to use it.** Atmospheric, emotional or night-time stories, dreamy intros, and anything that should feel hand-crafted and rich rather than clean.
