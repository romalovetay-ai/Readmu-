# Sticky-Note Wall

**Family:** Illustration · **id:** `sticky-notes`

**Look.** A brainstorming cork board in a dark wooden frame, the cork full of light and dark granules and old pin
holes, with an inner shadow under the frame. Post-its in yellow, pink, blue, green and orange each have a glossy
adhesive band at the top, a lower-right corner that curls up (with its darker underside and a wider, softer
shadow), and marker handwriting. Headlines go on a ruled index card held by two strips of translucent tape. Push
pins have a specular highlight and a cast shadow; red yarn sags between them with a twisted highlight.

**Motion.** Notes slap onto the board: they arrive slightly large and high (big soft shadow), land, and settle
with a tiny spring. Marker writing appears letter by letter with per-letter wobble. Yarn unspools pin to pin and
a pin is pressed in when it arrives. Scene changes are a whip pan to a fresh stretch of cork.

**How it explains.** A detective board: one note per idea, yarn for every link, the count written on an index card
where earlier milestones get crossed out in red as it climbs, and a red marker loop around the result.

**Palette.** cork `#b98a57`, frame `#6b4325`, marker ink `#1d2430`, blue marker `#1f4fa8`, red `#d2312a`,
yarn `#c3262b`, card `#fbf8f0`, notes `#f7e463` `#f59bbd` `#8fd0f0` `#a6e07a` `#f8b25c`.

**Fonts.** Permanent Marker (headlines, numbers, note titles), Caveat 700 / 600 (small handwriting).

**Signature techniques.**
- Cork baked once from tens of thousands of tiny two-tone crumbs over a radial base, plus pin holes and the frame.
- Note silhouette with a quadratic curl; drop shadow whose blur and offset scale with how far the note is from
  the board (the slap); a linear gradient for the adhesive band and the lifting bottom.
- Yarn: a sagging bezier, drawn as cast shadow + body + dashed highlight to read as twisted fibre.

**SFX.** draw `marker` scribbles timed to the writing, land `paper` + soft `thud` per note, `click` per pin,
emphasis marker scribble + light `stamp`, transition `whoosh` (whip pan), bed `room`.

**When to use it.** Brainstorms, planning, "how we mapped it out", retros, roadmaps, anything that should feel
collaborative and in-progress.
